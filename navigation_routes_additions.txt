No manual navigation-route addition is required when using the included App.tsx.

App.tsx already registers:
  ServiceHistory -> ServiceHistoryScreen
  NotificationScreen -> NotificationScreen

The included src/navigation/AppNavigator.tsx also registers both routes.

Exact route names used by the screens:
  navigation.navigate('ServiceHistory', { role: 'customer' | 'mechanic' | 'b2b' | 'driver' })
  navigation.navigate('NotificationScreen', { role: 'customer' | 'mechanic' | 'b2b' | 'driver' })
